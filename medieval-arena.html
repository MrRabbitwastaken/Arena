A duel in plate harness

Medieval Arena

One ring of stone, one opponent, one greatsword. The steel has weight and the arms have limits. A guard holds only if the blades actually meet, and plate turns an edge unless you find the gaps.

How to fight

Weight, timing, gaps

Cutting

Flick the mouse the way you want the blade to travel, then click. Down is an overhead, sideways is a horizontal cut, diagonals give descending and rising cuts. Click with the mouse still for a thrust. Hold the button to wind up harder: more force, more stamina, more time for him to read it.

Guarding

Hold right mouse and move the mouse to put your blade where the blow will land: towards the side it comes from, high for an overhead, low for a rising cut. Nothing is blocked unless the blades meet. Tap right mouse just before impact to parry, which throws his sword wide and gives you a fast counter.

Armour

Edges glance off good plate, though the blow still dents it and hurts the man inside. Cuts and thrusts bite at the neck, the armpits, the inside of the elbows and the backs of the knees, or through plate you have already battered.
